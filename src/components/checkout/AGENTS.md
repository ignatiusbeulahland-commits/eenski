- Checkout runs Order → Payment → Summary → Done in both the site checkout and the learner dashboard checkout; Summary is its own `?step=summary` address. Payment panels only collect details and hand back a `PayDecision`, kept (label only, never card numbers) in `lib/checkout/summary.ts` so refresh/back work; the shared `PaymentSummary` screen (lines, total, method with Change, receipt, terms, "Confirm and pay", card OTP after it) places the payment, and without a saved choice the step falls back to Payment. Why: the buyer reviews everything once before anything is charged, and no panel can pay on its own.
- A successful payment by a signed-in learner whose email matches is bridged into their library by `lib/learner/shopBridge.ts` (purchase + `shopLibrary` resource), and storefront items (`store:<handle>:<itemId>` keys) are credited once per payment to that creator's saved dashboard by `lib/creator/publicStore.ts`. Why: what you buy opens straight away and the seller sees the sale, without a backend.
