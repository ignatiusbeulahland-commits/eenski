- Identity Verification settings pages (Affiliate, Creator) compose presentational `VerificationKit.tsx` on top of `SettingsKit.tsx`; each role keeps its own state and validation. Why: one premium verification layout without shared state.
- Brand pop-ups (log out, profile switch) open with `BrandDialogHero` from `BrandDialog.tsx`, and every role uses the shared `LogoutDialog`. Why: one branded confirmation look in every role.
- Not-found, didn't-load and offline wording lives in `src/lib/shared/statusCopy.ts` and renders through `StatusScreen.tsx` (`MissingScreen`, `LoadErrorScreen`); each role's boundary only supplies its own links. Why: every missing or broken moment reads intentionally and identically across roles.
- Desktop sidebars (lg+) carry `.desk-rail` (styles in `src/styles.css`) and end with `SidebarDock.tsx` rendered by each role's profile menu via its `dock` prop; below lg the drawer and top bar keep account, theme and log out. Why: desktop gets its own sidebar look while phones and tablets keep the compact top bar.
- Pop-ups, side panels and confirmations share the round `closeButtonClass` close button from `dashboard-ui/dialog.tsx`; form pop-ups end with `DialogActions.tsx` (labelled Cancel beside the main action); the notification centre has its own brand header with a close button. Why: every pop-up shows a visible way out and a clearly named Cancel.
- Removals use `undoToast` (`src/lib/shared/undo.ts`) with `removeWhere`/`restoreAt`/`restoreAll` (`src/lib/shared/listUndo.ts`) so Undo re-inserts into the current list; irreversible work (deleting stored files) runs in `onCommit`, and anything with linked data (landing page + leads, programmes) also confirms first. Orders, payouts and transactions are never deletable. Why: mistakes are recoverable everywhere without rewriting later edits or financial history.
- Hidden items (Learner library `hiddenResources`, Creator `notificationsDismissed`) only change what's shown; ownership and the underlying records stay. Why: hiding must never cost access or history.
- Dialog focus: `useReturnFocus` (`src/lib/keyboard.ts`) returns focus to the opener, or to `#main-content` when the opener was removed. Why: keyboard users never land at the top of the document.
- Confirmations (archive, delete, remove, refund, disconnect, reset) use `ConfirmDialog` + `ConfirmNote` from `BrandDialog.tsx` with a 3D `brandArtwork` tile; Cancel takes focus first and `onConfirm` returning false keeps it open. Why: every role's yes/no choices share one brand design and never confirm by accident.
- Dashboard motion and loading: easing/timing tokens, `.dash-shimmer` and image fade rules live in `src/styles.css`; `DashboardFrame` runs `lib/dashboard/imageLoading` so every dashboard image lazy-loads and fades in; `Skeleton` uses the shimmer. Why: one motion and loading language across roles.
