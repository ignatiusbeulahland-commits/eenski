- The browseable catalogue (`src/lib/learner/catalog.ts`) is sample content; events and resources not in `publishedEvents`/`publishedResources` render only under the Preview pages view (`PreviewSwitch`) and are fully buyable/registrable so the whole learner journey can be tried, with a `PreviewBanner` step tracker; ownership, registrations and achievements derive only from local learner actions. Why: honest first-time states.
- Learner event times are scheduled in WAT and displayed in the viewer's zone via `useViewerTimeZone` (`src/lib/learner/timezone.ts`, renders WAT until hydrated); event details always also show the WAT time. Why: correct local times without hydration mismatches.
- The learning space (`components/learner/LessonPlayer` + `CourseQuiz`) reads modules, lesson details, drip locks and the creator's final quiz from the resource itself via pure `lib/learner/learning.ts`; a required quiz gates `resourceProgress().complete` (and so the certificate), and storefront-course progress and best quiz score are mirrored into the creator's order by `syncStoreLearning`. Why: one source for lesson states, honest completion, and creator and learner see the same progress.
