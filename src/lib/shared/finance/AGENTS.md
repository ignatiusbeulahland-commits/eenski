- Payouts, saved beneficiaries, wallets and currency settings for every role compose `src/components/shared/finance/*` over pure rules in `src/lib/shared/finance/*` (provider coverage, Paystack bank directory snapshot, validators, per-currency balances, ExchangeRate-API rates); roles keep their own lists and prefs and sync the preferred account into their legacy `bank` field. Why: one financial design system without shared state, and older screens keep working.
- Beneficiary changes go through each store's `commit()` (writes storage first, applies only on success) so the UI never reports a save that failed. Why: user requires no pretend saves.
- Every role shows money movements through shared `TxHistory` (rows built by pure role mappers such as `creatorTransactions`/`learnerTransactions` into `TxRow`), payout facts through `PayoutInfo`, and the balance privacy toggle via `useBalancePrivacy`. Why: one transaction and payout presentation; statuses map from real records only.
- Bank lists split into Commercial / Microfinance / Other via `institutionKind` (`coverage.ts`): Nigeria uses the directory's CBN licence group, other countries count as microfinance only when the name says so; unclear institutions go to Other, never guessed. The add-account flow keeps its actions in a sticky bottom bar and opens as a bottom sheet on phones (`phoneSheetClass`). Why: no misclassified banks, and Continue can't scroll out of view.
- `TxHistory` totals come from pure `txSummary` (one line per currency, failed rows excluded, refunds kept separate) and every row with an `href` opens its details anywhere on the row; refunded sales use the `refunded` status and creator refunds go through `RefundSaleDialog` (`components/creator/Finance.tsx`), which saves `refundedAt` and opens the sale's details. Why: totals never mix currencies, and a refund ends on a screen that shows it.
