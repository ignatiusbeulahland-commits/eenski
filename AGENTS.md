## Project rules
- Dreamverse creator discovery merges catalogue Enski profiles ahead of Nigerian previews by slug (live status wins); the directory is the one-at-a-time `CreatorDeck` in `components/dreamverse/CreatorDiscovery`. Why: no duplicate cards or false partnership claims.
- Homepage sections live in `src/components/site/`, one file per section; shared copy, catalog and links live in `site/data.ts` so content edits don't touch layout.
- Cut-out people are pre-processed transparent WebP files in `src/assets/people/`; Stories avatars and the Community member image stay as real stock photos.
- Brand colours are oklch tokens in `src/styles.css`; components use those tokens and `Button` variants (`lime`, `outline-light`, etc.) rather than raw colour classes.
- Newsletter signups are simulated in `site/newsletter.ts`: saved on the device only via `lib/demo/local.ts` (no email is sent); emails are trimmed and lower-cased so a repeat reads as "already on the list", and the optional first name is trimmed (blank saves as null).
- Brand clothing on people is done offline: old marks are removed by filling only the logo area with matching fabric, then the official logo file is stamped with the fabric's folds, light and texture (print or embroidery, sized and placed per garment); face, hands and props stay original photo pixels. Why: logos must read as real garment branding, not pasted stickers.
- Light/dark theme mirrors the Tryenski Com project: a `.dark` class on `<html>` set before paint by `themeInitScript` (saved choice, else system), toggled by `site/ThemeToggle`. Text on page surfaces uses `text-ink` / `text-brand` (they flip in dark); keep `text-midnight` only on lime or other fixed-colour surfaces.
- Every product listing (homepage, `/courses`, Dreamverse shelves, storefronts, `/wishlist` and its panel, related items) renders `components/shop` cards in `ShopGrid`/`ShopShelf` (grid + "Show more", no side-scrolling rails) via `shop/adapters.ts`, at least two per row even on phones; never on checkout or details bodies. Wishlist, cart and recently viewed are per device in `lib/shop/store.ts`. Why: one card look, and ratings/sales only when real.
- Course/ebook covers: real Unsplash photos, light brand grade (`assets/courses/CREDITS.md`), never AI; no faces for real instructors. People cut-outs are AI-generated on grey, then garments are recoloured to exact brand hex and stamped with the real Enski logo offline.
- Motion mirrors the Tryenski Com project via `site/motion.tsx` (`Reveal` scroll-in that replays both ways, `RiseWords` headline, `CountUp` figures) and the motion CSS in `src/styles.css` (`.reveal`, `.word-rise`, `animate-rise`, `animate-bob`, `animate-float`, `parallax-y`); new sections should reuse these, and everything must stay still under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Hero and creator photos share `site/PeopleSlides` and `slide-loading`: advance only after decode, warm only the next visible section photo after page load, and stop autoplay offscreen, during interaction, on low data or reduced motion. Why: consistent pacing without blank slides or competing first-screen downloads.
- The top menu mirrors the Enski Blog project's header (dropdowns for For Creators and Resources); its items live in `mainNav` in `site/data.ts` so menu edits stay out of `SiteHeader`.
- `/auth` (`?s=register|forgot`, `?as=creator|affiliate`) signs into a tab-only demo profile (`lib/demo/profile.ts`) and opens that role's dashboard. Dashboards `/learner|creator|affiliate` (from Enski internal Dashboard; own `dashboard-ui`, `assets/dashboard`) run inside `shared/DashboardFrame` (`data-skin="dashboard"`, sign-in required) and save per email via `lib/dashboard/account.ts`. Why: new users start empty with their own name.
- Bundled images are pre-sized offline to about 2x their largest on-screen size and saved as WebP (no PNG/JPG in `src/assets` imports); hero slides add 640/960w copies with measured `sizes` (`site/hero-images.ts`, shared with the `/` preload and `loadSlide`); only slide 1 loads eagerly, the rest after page load.
- Imported sibling projects are local pages reusing `site/SiteHeader`, `SiteFooter` and `Newsletter`, each with its own `components/<page>` and `assets/<page>/`: `/about`, `/pricing`, `/blog` (+`content/blog`), `/features` (Creators.tryenski), `/dreamverse` + `/creators/$slug` (`lib/dreamverse`), `/community`, `/faq` (Help Center), `/become-an-ambassador` hub + `_/campus`, `_/nysc` (`ambassador/kit`, `lib/ambassador`), `/dreamers-trybe` (`lib/dreamers-trybe`). Why: one menu and footer, each page refreshable from its source.
- `/features`, Dreamverse and `/faq` pages wrap `<main>` in `data-skin="features|dreamverse|help"`, which resets corner sizes (`--r-*`), `--on-blue`, `--accent` and shadows to their source projects; they also keep their own `button.tsx`, and Dreamverse's headline utilities are renamed `dv-*`. Why: those projects used different tokens and button variants than the rest of the site.
- The Plus Jakarta Sans font is self-hosted via `@fontsource-variable/plus-jakarta-sans` (imported in `src/styles.css`, Latin file preloaded in `__root`), never Google Fonts. Why: no third-party round trip, and it works offline.
- Connection and loading states live in `components/site/network/` (mounted once in `__root`), logic in `lib/network/`: `monitor.ts` alone listens to online/offline, pings uncached `/robots.txt` and watches same-origin fetch failures; recovery re-runs failed data (`router.invalidate`, errored queries), never reloads. Splash shows once per tab session (CSS fallback synced with the timing module); slow navigations use the top bar + `PageSkeleton`. Why: one source of truth, no reload loops, no full-screen loader on navigation.
- Buy/enroll buttons all use `site/checkout/BuyGate`'s `BuyButton`, which opens the one sitewide simulated checkout (`shop/DemoCheckout` in `__root`, via `openCheckout` in `lib/shop/panel.ts`) with no sign-up step; paying records a demo order on the device (cart "Check out" reuses it; orders show in the header panel's Orders tab). Why: one buying flow, no real payments.
- Listing pages (course, eBook, coaching…) share `components/product/ProductLayout` + `parts`; each page only maps its data in, and Enski-style `/course|ebooks|coaching/<creator>/<slug>` links redirect to them. Why: one look, no duplicate pages.
- Ratings and reviews are demo data kept on the device (`lib/reviews`, keyed `course|showcase|dreamverse:<slug>`), shown by `product/ProductReviews` on every details page and as card ratings; one review per email per listing, emails never shown. No made-up reviews are preloaded. Why: frontend-only prototype, and ratings must stay honest.
- Details pages pick a display country/currency via `product/PricePicker` (`lib/pricing`): a creator-set price in that currency is shown exactly; anything else is a labelled "≈" estimate from the saved demo rates in `lib/pricing/demo-rates.ts` (dated, credited), and checkout uses the creator's own price. The country defaults to the browser language's region; the choice is saved per device. Why: familiar money without live rate lookups or invented prices.
- Enski is a frontend-only prototype: no Lovable Cloud/Supabase client, server functions, API routes or live Enski/third-party data calls. Catalogue and course pages come from the saved snapshot in `src/content/demo/*.json` (photos in `public/demo/enski/`) via `lib/dreamverse/data.ts` and `lib/marketplace/data.ts`; simulated actions save on the device. `src/integrations/` is regenerated by the platform while Cloud stays on: excluded from typecheck, never imported. Why: the site must open, demo and work offline with no backend.
- Charm + simulated support: rules `lib/assistant` (local keywords, no AI; device stores shared with `/support/*`), UI `components/assistant` (once in `__root`, hidden on focused journeys; `openCharm()`), staff desk `components/support`, role help at `/<role>/help/*` (`components/help-centre`, `lib/help-centre`). Chats are threads in the panel; help pages only reopen them. Why: frontend-only; no navigation takeover.
- Creator facilitation (coaching, live, services) comes from pure `lib/creator/bookings.ts` for `/creator/bookings` and the dashboard. Why: one source, no facilitator role.
- Creator certificate branding (logos, seal, artwork, signatories) is a per-account brand kit in `lib/creator/brandKit.ts`, copied into a course's certificate design on "Apply kit"; uploads go through `lib/shared/brandImage.ts` (type/size checks, transparency kept). Why: upload once, reuse on every course without stretching images.
- Fixed and floating elements take a named stacking layer from `src/styles.css` (`--z-sticky` … `--z-splash`, used as `z-(--z-float)`), never a raw z-index number; component-library overlays keep their default `z-50` (= `--z-overlay`). Why: Charm, sticky bars, dialogs and status screens stay in one predictable order.
- `docs/refinement-checklist.md` holds the Batch 1 audit (keep-list, design inconsistencies, open items per area); later batches tick items there instead of starting new audit files. Why: one running list across the five refinement batches.
- Major journeys (checkout, withdrawals, beneficiaries, certificate/badge issue and award, sign-up/log-in, publish, support) end on their own result routes built from `components/shared/outcome` (`OutcomeScreen`, `ProcessingScreen`, `FlowSteps`, `FocusShell`); toasts are only for minor actions. Why: every important action leads to a screen with details and a next step.
- The `/admin` console (tab-only sign-in in `lib/admin/session.ts`) reads every role's saved data read-only via pure `lib/admin/data.ts`; its only writes are `lib/admin/store.ts` (suspensions, read marks, settings) and `revokeAsEnski` in the credentials registry, and `/auth` refuses suspended emails. Why: one honest overview without changing role stores.
- Sign-up → `/welcome` → `/onboarding?step=n` (`lib/onboarding`) → role dashboard. Roles are learner, creator, affiliate only; facilitators are creators, and old saved or linked "facilitator" values map to creator via `joinRoleFrom`. Why: resumable setup with no separate facilitator role.
